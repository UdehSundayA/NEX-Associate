👨‍🏫 NEX Associate — System Architecture & Role

NEX Associate is the teacher-facing logistics, distribution, customer success, and delivery gateway for Nexus Learning. Operating as the distribution client, NEX Associate queries NEX Curriculum (the backend content engine) to fetch ready-to-teach 13-Section Lesson Plans, 5-Step Blackboard Notes, Flashcards, and WAEC/IGCSE Test Kits.

1. PROFILE & BILLING

Teacher selects Curriculum (NERDC/Cambridge/American), Class level, and Paid Subjects billed per-subject.

2. QUERY & DISTRIBUTE

Queries NEX Curriculum REST API to fetch manufactured kits and delivers via Dashboard, WhatsApp, and PDF/Word exports.

3. FEEDBACK RELAY

Forwards teacher comments to NEX Curriculum for updates, relaying resolution messages back to teachers.

💳 Teacher Profile & Per-Subject Subscription Billing

Teachers configure their Curriculum Framework, select their active Class Level, and choose the Subjects they teach. Teachers are billed transparently on a per-subject subscription model (₦1,500 / $3.00 per subject / month) with volume discounts.
